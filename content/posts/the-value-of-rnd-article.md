Research and Development AKA R&D is a common branch in many big companies dedicated to **“playing around”** with ideas until they strike something good. 

> **Experimentation is not treated as an afterthought, but as a necessity for the survival of the company.**

Companies have a metric called **“R&D Intensity”**, the ratio of revenue spent on R&D. The riskier, or “growth oriented” the company is, the bigger the R&D intensity. Biotech is naturally a leader in the field.

Our personal R&D is very important, since being a passionate person with lots of ideas is a pretty risky business. We often get distracted by our daily tasks and responsibilities, that we forget that some personal R&D could make the same tasks a lot easier. **Permanently.**

Spending some time to solve a recurring problem is **always** right. There is a cap to the amount of work you can do in a day, but optimizing that work **multiplies** that number. Every little improvement moves the needle much further than you initially realize.

### DRY

There is a programming principle called [DRY(Don’t Repeat Yourself)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t**repeat**yourself). It states that if you start repeating code, you should package it and **reuse the whole thing** , instead of writing it manually every time. Well, the files you refuse to organize, that skill you want to learn that is just a **tad too overwhelming** right now, or trying a new way of folding clothes are all different ways of **not** **repeating yourself.** Hence, **find a way to do it once.**

### **Grab the opportunity**

You don’t have to set aside special R&D time, just be ready to grab the opportunity when it presents itself. Let your idea blossom, instead of shutting it down because you “really don’t wanna bother right now”. The results are transforming.

## For anyone using a computer in their day-to-day
* * *

### Touch typing

[Learn to touch type.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch**typing) I can’t state this enough, this has saved me so much time and mental stress. Every key you have to search on your keyboard drains a tiny bit of your mental energy and all of it accumulates quickly. If you use more than one language, get used to touch typing both. Learning doesn’t have to be boring though, check out these websites/games:

  * [Keybr](https://www.keybr.com/) (start here)

  * [Nitrotype](https://www.nitrotype.com/)

  * [Monkeytype](https://monkeytype.com/)

  * [Glyphica: Typing Survival](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2400160/Glyphica**Typing**Survival/)

### Linux

Play around with Linux. Boot up a virtual machine, dual boot or just flash an old laptop. You can ask a tech savvy friend for help, if the task is daunting. Linux has improved drastically the past couple of years and we have reached the point of surpassing Windows in many aspects. You won’t regret it. Give these distros a spin:

  * [Fedora KDE Plasma](https://fedoraproject.org/kde/)

  * [Mint Cinnamon](https://www.linuxmint.com/)

  * [Zorin OS](https://zorin.com/os/)

  * [Ubuntu](https://ubuntu.com/download/desktop)


## Software developer exclusives
* * *

### LINUX. THIS IS NOT A SUGGESTION. DO IT. 

Even better, play around with tiling window managers, such a game changer. My daily driver for about half an year is [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/), been treating me great. Comes pre-installed with [Hyprland](https://hypr.land/).

Update from a later date - Omarchy became a bit unstable and too "agentic" for my tastes, so I switched to [Fedora KDE Plasma](https://fedoraproject.org/kde/) with [Noctalia](https://noctalia.dev/) and [KineticWE](https://gitlab.com/theblackdon/kineticwe). The Arch journey has been fun, but quite unstable and I just want my stuff to work now. Hate to admit it but I always had something wrong on my Arch setup and required too much time to maintain.

### The Vim Workflow

[This is Vim.](https://youtu.be/-txKSRn0qeA?si=ss2oLiJe7-1RfrZk) The video showcases vim usage in VSCode, as well as the standard vim terminal. This deserves a whole article, but briefly - you can integrate vim in a lot more than your text editor of choice:

  * [Your browser](https://vimium.github.io/) (Vimium)

  * [PDF reader](https://pwmt.org/projects/zathura/) (Zathura)

  * [File explorer](https://yazi-rs.github.io/) (Yazi)

  * [Zed](https://zed.dev/) editor has integrated vim support

  * [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) has integrated vim support

  * Godot has a [vim plugin](https://godotengine.org/asset-library/asset/4666)

  * My [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/) configuration has some vim keybinds

I daily drive these, the browser plugin and file explorer had the most impact. Learn vim and a tiling window manager and ditch your mouse completely. **Incredible bragging rights.**

Share to someone you’ll brag to. They just gotta know.

*This would not be possible without some heavy R&D.*
